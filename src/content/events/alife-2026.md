ALIFE 2026 is the annual conference of the International Society for Artificial
Life. The main conference sessions take place at Wilfrid Laurier University,
with additional events at the University of Waterloo and nearby locations.

See the [official ALIFE 2026 website](https://2026.alife.org/) for programme and
attendance information.
